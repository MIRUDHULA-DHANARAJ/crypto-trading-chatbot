GENERAL CRYPTO KNOWLEDGE
==================================================
Cryptocurrency:
A digital asset that uses cryptographic techniques and blockchain or distributed-ledger technology.

Blockchain:
A distributed digital ledger maintained by a network of computers.

Coin vs Token:
A coin normally operates on its own native blockchain. A token is generally issued on an existing blockchain.

Market Cap:
Market capitalization is approximately price × circulating supply.

Liquidity:
How easily an asset can be bought or sold without causing a large price impact.

Volatility:
The degree to which an asset's price changes over time. Crypto can be highly volatile.

Stablecoin:
A crypto asset designed to maintain a relatively stable value against another asset, commonly the US dollar.

DeFi:
Decentralized finance applications that provide financial functions using blockchain-based protocols and smart contracts.

Smart Contract:
A program deployed on a blockchain that automatically executes predefined logic.

Wallet:
Software or hardware used to manage cryptographic keys and interact with blockchain networks.

Public Key / Address:
Information used to receive blockchain assets.

Private Key:
Secret cryptographic information that controls access to assets. Never share it.

Seed Phrase:
A secret recovery phrase used to restore a crypto wallet. Never share it with anyone.
