# Bitcoin Basics

## What is Bitcoin?

Bitcoin is a decentralized digital currency and payment network. It allows users to transfer value directly over a peer-to-peer network without requiring a central bank or other central authority to process every transaction.

Bitcoin's software and design are open source, meaning its code and protocol can be publicly inspected.

## What is the Bitcoin blockchain?

The Bitcoin blockchain is a shared public ledger containing confirmed Bitcoin transactions.

Transactions are grouped into blocks, and blocks are connected chronologically to form a chain. Cryptographic techniques help protect the integrity of the transaction history.

The blockchain allows participants in the Bitcoin network to independently verify transactions and determine the state of the network.

## What is a Bitcoin wallet?

A Bitcoin wallet is software or hardware that allows a user to manage Bitcoin addresses and authorize transactions.

A wallet does not literally contain coins. Instead, it manages the cryptographic information needed to control Bitcoin associated with particular addresses.

## What is a Bitcoin address?

A Bitcoin address is an identifier that can be used to receive Bitcoin.

Users can share an address with someone who wants to send them Bitcoin. For privacy and security reasons, users may generate different addresses for different transactions.

## What is a private key?

A private key is secret cryptographic information that allows a user to authorize Bitcoin transactions.

A transaction can be digitally signed using the private key. The network can then verify that the transaction was authorized by the holder of the corresponding key.

A private key should never be shared with another person.

## What is Bitcoin mining?

Bitcoin mining is the process used to confirm transactions and add new blocks to the Bitcoin blockchain.

Miners compete to produce blocks that satisfy the network's cryptographic requirements. The mining process helps the distributed network agree on the order of transactions and makes it computationally difficult to modify previously confirmed blocks.

## What is a Bitcoin transaction?

A Bitcoin transaction is a transfer of Bitcoin from one or more inputs to one or more outputs.

Transactions are broadcast to the Bitcoin network. Valid transactions can eventually be included in a block and become part of the blockchain.

## What is decentralization?

Decentralization means that Bitcoin is not controlled by a single central organization.

Instead, many independent computers participate in maintaining and verifying the network. This distributed structure means users can verify the system according to the Bitcoin protocol rather than relying entirely on one central authority.

## Bitcoin and trading

Bitcoin can be traded against traditional currencies or other cryptocurrencies on cryptocurrency exchanges.

Common trading concepts include:

* Market orders
* Limit orders
* Stop-loss orders
* Trading volume
* Price volatility
* Support and resistance
* Technical indicators
* Risk management

The price of Bitcoin can change rapidly. Past price performance does not guarantee future results.

## Important safety note

Bitcoin trading involves financial risk. A knowledge chatbot should provide educational information rather than personalized financial advice or guaranteed predictions.

The chatbot should not claim that a user will make a profit or recommend a specific trade without appropriate context and safeguards.
